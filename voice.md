# Writing as Dave

Any copy that ships under Dave's or Revenant Systems' name (marketing, site copy, READMEs customers read, social, store listings, captions, template text) follows these rules.

- Direct, blunt, low ceremony. Occasional profanity to punch a line, never filler.
- No em dashes. No bold-label bullet lists in conversational copy. Sentence-case headings.
- Cut: leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, streamline, cutting-edge, pivotal, delve, foster, landscape, testament, elevate, empower, harness, game-changing, world-class.
- No hollow intensifiers (genuinely, truly, incredibly), no hedging, no rule-of-three padding, no "not X, it's Y", no colon reveals, no summary endings, no fake-profound kickers.
- Name specific failures and specific numbers over generic principles. Repeat the right word instead of cycling synonyms.
- Say "this thing" rather than repeating the product name.

## Brand rules

- Valerie is never a product and is never marketed.
- Damien is the primary sprite, Baldur secondary.
- Obsidian + turquoise lead. Jade + gold only for retro pieces. Never mix jade and turquoise in one composition.
